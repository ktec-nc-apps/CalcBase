CalcBase quick manual
CalcBase is a spreadsheet that runs inside Nextcloud. A book is kept in Files as an ordinary .html file, which any browser opens and shows.
Formulas and functions are written as in LibreOffice Calc and Excel. Where the two differ, CalcBase follows Calc.
The screen
The list on the leftBooks and categories. Drag a book onto a category to move it there. Right-click for rename, share, delete and more.
+ New book / +CSVMakes an empty book. +CSV makes one from a CSV, ODS, XLSX or Markdown table in your Files.
The bar at the topSave, Print / PDF, Web preview, Paper setup, View the HTML, and More (…).
The rows of toolsCell style, typeface and size, bold and the rest, colours, borders, alignment, merging, number formats.
The rail on the leftInsert (functions, rows and columns, pictures, links, comments, names), Bring in, Data (sort, autofilter), and what is shown.
The strip on the rightOpens and shuts the AI assistant, and the sheet bar with a small picture of each sheet.
The footThe sheet tabs (double-click to rename) and the sum, average and count of the selected cells.

Bring in and print

Bringing in, exporting, printing
Bring in (the rail on the left)
RegiBaseChoose a collection: a sheet with a row per record and a column per field. Dates stay dates and numbers numbers. Secret fields never come in.
FormulaBaseA sheet of formulas, their variables and results. A result comes in as a live formula wherever it can be one.
EditBaseThe tables of a document, one sheet each, their formulas kept.
NetBase, TablesThe devices NetBase has found, or one of your Tables, as a sheet.
Contacts, CalendarYour contacts, or the events of a stretch of days, as a sheet.
A web pageType its address: every table on the page comes in, one sheet each.
A fileA CSV, ODS, XLSX or Markdown table in your Files.
Where it goes"Add as new sheets" or "Put it at the cursor". Either way one Ctrl+Z takes it out again.
Export (…)
CSV, ODS, XLSXWritten next to the book. Formulas, values, number formats, styles, column widths and merges are kept.
Download a copySaves the book's .html file to your computer.
Printing
Print / PDFThe used range, the selection, or every sheet. For a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" in the browser's print dialogue.
Paper setupPaper, orientation and margins, header and footer, scaling (fit to the page width, or to a number of pages). It is saved in the book.
Header and footer{page} is the page number, {pages} how many there are, {sheet} the sheet's name, {title} the book's, {date} the day it was printed.
Page breaksUnder View on the rail: shows where the pages will fall.
Saving and sharing
SavingWhat you type is saved as you go (the settings can switch this off). Ctrl+S saves at once.
VersionsEach save can keep the earlier book beside it (#01 to #99). Put one back from "Versions…" under More.
SharingRight-click a book or a category and choose "Share…": with other people on this server, to read or to write.
Check the bookUnder More: looks for error values, formulas pointing at empty cells, numbers kept as text and columns wider than the paper.

AI assistant

The AI assistant
Open "AI assistant" on the strip at the right to ask CalcBase's own AI questions, or to ask it to do things (when the administrator allows it).
Each question goes to the AI together with the used range of the open sheet and what is selected.
What the AI writes into cells comes off again with one Ctrl+Z.
The apps the administrator allows (RegiBase and the others) are read only when the AI needs them, and the AI never changes them.
Things to ask
Working out"Put the total of B2:B10 in B11"
Explaining"What does the formula in column D do?"
Building"Add rows for the average and the largest value under this table"
How to"How do I sort by sales, largest first?"
About a cellRight-click it and choose "Ask the assistant about this cell".
